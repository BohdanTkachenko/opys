# The omnibox: a fuzzy finder over tickets, from the header

The search field is gone from the board's toolbar. In its place the header
carries a placeholder that looks like a search field and is a button: clicking
it — or Ctrl/⌘+P, or `/` — opens the omnibox, a fuzzy finder over tickets. Scope
is the corpus on screen, or every served corpus from the home page and the
union view (rows then carry a corpus chip). Before a character is typed the
list is "what moved lately", by `updated`.

Typing ranks with a small fzf-style scorer (`lib/fuzzy.js`): a contiguous hit
outranks scattered ones, word starts and adjacency score up, gaps cost; the id
counts most, then the title, then tags and status; matches are highlighted.
Up/Down (or Ctrl+N/Ctrl+P) move, Enter opens, Escape or a click outside
closes. On a board the last row applies the text as the board's filter (`?q=`,
shown as a chip in the toolbar) — what the old search field did; the box
replaced it, so it keeps the ability. Documents are fetched when the box
opens, one request per corpus in scope, so it shows the corpus as it is now.
