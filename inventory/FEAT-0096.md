# The narrow-screen shell folds the sidebar into a bar

On a screen under 46rem the shell stacks, and the sidebar used to stack in
full — corpus list, verify problems, version, last load — so every view began
a third of the way down and a board's first card sat below the fold. It is now
a one-line bar: the wordmark (home), the live dot, and a projects toggle
carrying the served count. The toggle opens the same list in place
(`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`); any navigation folds it back, so a tap on
a corpus lands on its board and not on the list that led there. The footer is
desktop furniture and stays hidden on the bar.

Nothing else changes: the same component, one piece of state, and CSS that
comes *last* in its sheet on purpose — it overrides rules of equal specificity
above it, and the cascade settles ties by order (the footer kept showing until
it did).
