# Modification heat and relation badges on the board

Two per-card signals that make a busy board legible and *live*:

- **Relation badges**: each board card shows its live blocker counts —
  `blocked_by` as a red lock badge, `blocks n` as a whisper — computed
  server-side in `DocSummary` from the relation maps, struck tombstones
  excluded. Blockers are the corpus's load-bearing relation and were invisible
  on the board before this.
- **Modification heat**: cards recently written glow with a faint amber ring
  that cools over the day, driven by the `updated` timestamp the engine stamps
  on every write. With the event stream reloading the board live, an agent
  working through the inventory leaves a visible warm trail. Deliberately built
  on mtime, not atime: `relatime`/`noatime` mounts make access times a lie on
  most machines (see the activity-feed feature for the honest way to get
  opens).
