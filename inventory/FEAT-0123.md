# The wide-screen sidebar folds into a rail

Above 46rem the sidebar was a fixed 17rem column, so a laptop or a half-width
window gave a sixth of its width to a project list and squeezed the board into
two and a half columns. It now folds into a rail: a button in its header (and
`[` from anywhere outside a text field) collapses it to the wordmark, the live
dot and the unfold button, and the main column takes the rest of the width.

The choice persists in `localStorage` (`opys:sidebar:collapsed`). With nothing
stored, a viewport under 64rem starts folded and a wider one starts open. The
narrow-screen bar of [FEAT-0096 — The narrow-screen shell folds the sidebar into a bar](FEAT-0096.md) is unchanged and ignores the preference: a fold
chosen on a wide window must not empty the bar on a phone.
